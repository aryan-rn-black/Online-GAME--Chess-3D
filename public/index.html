♔ The King

The most important piece on the board. The entire game revolves around protecting your King while threatening your opponent's. When the King is trapped with no escape, the game is over.

MOVEMENT

Moves one square in any direction: horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

VALUE

Infinite - The game ends if captured

TIP

Keep your King safe behind a wall of pawns in the opening. Castle early to protect him.

♛ The Queen

The most powerful piece in chess, combining the movement of both the Rook and Bishop. A Queen in the center of the board can control up to 27 squares

MOVEMENT

Moves any number of squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

VALUE

9 points

TIP

Don't bring your Queen out too early - she can become a target for your opponent's minor pieces.

♖ The Rook

A powerful major piece that dominates open files and ranks. Two Rooks working together can deliver devastating attacks and are particularly strong in the endgame.

MOVEMENT

Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically.

VALUE

5 points

TIP

Activate your Rooks by placing them on open files. Connected Rooks on the 7th rank are extremely powerful.

♗ The Bishop

A long-range piece that can sweep across the board in an instant. Each player starts with two Bishops - one on light squares, one on dark squares. They never change color.

MOVEMENT

Moves any number of squares diagonally.

VALUE

3 points

TIP

The Bishop pair is powerful in open positions. Place Bishops on long diagonals for maximum impact.

♘ The Knight

The only piece that can jump over others. Its unique L-shaped movement makes it tricky to use but deadly in closed positions where other pieces struggle to maneuver.

MOVEMENT

Moves in an L-shape: two squares in one direction, then one square perpendicular.

VALUE

3 points

TIP

Knights are strongest in the center and weak on the edges. A Knight on the rim is dim!

♙ The Pawn

The soul of chess. Though individually weak, pawns form the structure around which all strategy is built. A pawn reaching the opposite end of the board transforms into any piece except the King.

MOVEMENT

Moves forward one square (two squares on first move). Captures diagonally.

VALUE

1 point

TIP

Pawn structure is crucial. Avoid creating isolated or doubled pawns that become weaknesses.
